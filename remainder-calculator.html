How it works

A remainder is what is left over after dividing one number by another. For example, 17 divided by 5 gives 3 with a remainder of 2. This calculator makes it easy to find the remainder instantly without performing the division by hand.

The tool is useful in math classes, coding, number theory, and everyday problem solving. It can help users check division answers, understand modular arithmetic, and solve questions involving groups, cycles, or repeating patterns.

Enter the dividend and divisor, then click calculate. The result will display both the quotient and the remainder so you can review the full division outcome. The interface is kept clean and responsive to match the rest of CalcTools Hub.
