Markup Calculator

Calculate markup amount, markup percentage, and final selling price.

Result:
Enter the cost price and markup percentage to see the selling price.

How to Use

Enter the original cost of the item and the markup percentage. The calculator will show the markup amount and the final selling price.

  • Markup Amount = Cost Price × Markup Percentage
  • Selling Price = Cost Price + Markup Amount
  • Useful for retail pricing and profit planning
Tip: If you need a different pricing formula, you can edit this page later to support dollar-based markup input too.
