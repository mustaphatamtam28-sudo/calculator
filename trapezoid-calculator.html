About This Tool

A trapezoid is a four-sided shape with one pair of parallel sides. It is often used in geometry, construction, and design calculations. This tool helps you calculate either the area or the perimeter of a trapezoid using a clean and simple interface that works well on mobile devices.

For area, enter the two bases and the height. The formula is area = (base1 + base2) ÷ 2 × height. For perimeter, enter all four side lengths and add them together. If you are studying geometry or solving practical measurement problems, this calculator gives you a quick result without extra steps.
