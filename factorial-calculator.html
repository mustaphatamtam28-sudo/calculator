About This Tool

The factorial of a number is written with an exclamation mark, such as 5!, and it means multiplying all whole numbers from that number down to 1. For example, 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120. Factorials are widely used in mathematics, statistics, probability, permutations, and combinations. This calculator gives you a fast way to compute factorial values without doing the multiplication manually.

Factorials grow very quickly, so even small numbers can produce huge results. That is why factorial calculations are useful in many math problems but can become extremely large after only a few steps. This tool is designed for simple use on mobile and desktop, with a clean layout and instant output. If you are studying combinatorics, algebra, or probability, factorials are one of the most important operations to know.

To use the calculator, enter a whole number starting from 0. The result for 0! is 1, which is a standard rule in mathematics. Negative numbers and decimal values are not valid for a basic factorial calculator like this one.
