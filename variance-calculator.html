About This Variance Calculator

The Variance Calculator helps you measure how spread out a data set is. Variance is a key concept in statistics because it shows how much the values differ from the average. A small variance means the data points are close together, while a large variance means the values are more spread out. This makes variance useful in school, research, business, and data analysis.

To use the calculator, enter your numbers separated by commas, spaces, or line breaks. Then choose whether you want sample variance or population variance. Sample variance is used when the data is only part of a larger group, while population variance is used when you have the full set. The calculator then finds the average, measures the distance of each value from the mean, squares those distances, and calculates the final variance.

Understanding variance can help you compare data sets, assess consistency, and prepare for more advanced statistics topics. It is often used with standard deviation, which is the square root of variance. Together, these tools help explain patterns in test scores, sales numbers, measurements, and many other forms of data.
