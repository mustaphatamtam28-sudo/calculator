About This Tool

A sum calculator adds multiple numbers together and gives you the total instantly. It is a simple but very useful tool for schoolwork, budgeting, shopping totals, and everyday arithmetic. Instead of adding each number by hand, you can paste a list of values and get the answer in one step.

This calculator accepts numbers separated by commas, spaces, or line breaks, so it is easy to use on both desktop and mobile devices. Whether you are working with small values or a long list of numbers, the tool gives a quick and clear result. It is especially helpful when you want to check homework or combine totals from a spreadsheet, receipt, or calculation note.
