How it works

This calculator uses the distance formula to measure the straight-line distance between two points on a plane. It is useful in geometry, mapping, design, physics, and any situation where you need to compare positions or measure separation in two dimensions.

Enter the x and y values for both points, then click calculate. The tool will return the exact distance between them. This makes it easy to verify homework answers, explore coordinate geometry, or work on planning tasks.
