About This Tool

A linear equation is one of the simplest types of algebraic equations. It describes a straight-line relationship between variables and usually has the form ax + b = c. This calculator solves for x when you enter the values of a, b, and c. It is useful for students, teachers, and anyone who needs a fast algebra check.

Linear equations appear in many everyday math situations, including budgeting, pricing, distance calculations, and basic science formulas. Solving them by hand is easy once you know the process, but a calculator helps you confirm your work quickly. If a equals zero, the equation is no longer a standard linear equation, so the tool will alert you to that case.

Use this tool by entering the coefficient values and pressing calculate. The result will show the value of x, or explain when the equation has no unique solution.
