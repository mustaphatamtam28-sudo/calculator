How it works

This calculator measures the time gap between two selected dates and returns a clear breakdown of the result. It is useful for planning projects, counting event durations, checking deadlines, or comparing important milestones on a timeline. The tool is designed for quick everyday use and provides a clean output that is easy to understand on both mobile and desktop devices.

For best accuracy, choose the earlier date as the start date and the later date as the end date. The calculator will display the number of days between them, along with approximate weeks, months, and years. This makes it helpful for students, office work, travel planning, and personal record keeping.
