About This Tool

Significant figures show how precise a measured or written number is. They help identify which digits matter when reporting values in science and math. This calculator counts the significant figures in a number entered as text, making it easy to check homework, lab work, and classroom problems without guessing.

Rules for significant figures can be tricky because zeros may or may not count depending on where they appear. Leading zeros are not significant, trailing zeros after a decimal usually are, and zeros between nonzero digits are significant. Because of these rules, a value like 0.00450 has three significant figures, while 1200 may have different interpretations depending on whether it is written with a decimal or in scientific notation.

Use this calculator when you need a quick answer for chemistry, physics, engineering, or general measurement problems. Type the number exactly as shown, then click the button to count the significant figures. If you work with scientific notation often, this tool can save time and help keep your answers consistent with standard math conventions.
