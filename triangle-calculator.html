About This Tool

Triangles are one of the most common shapes in geometry. They appear in construction, design, navigation, and many school math problems. This calculator helps with two of the most common triangle tasks: finding area from base and height, and finding perimeter from three side lengths.

To calculate area, enter the base and height, then use the formula area = 1/2 × base × height. To calculate perimeter, enter all three side lengths and add them together. The tool is designed to keep the process simple and clear so it works well on a phone or computer.

Triangle calculations are useful in classrooms and real-world planning. Whether you are checking homework, measuring a project, or solving a geometry problem, this page gives you a quick answer in a consistent CalcTools Hub layout. It is part of the broader set of math tools designed for fast, easy use.
